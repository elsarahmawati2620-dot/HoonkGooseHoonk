Who is $uGOOSE?

$uGOOSE is a generative collection of 3000 unique pixel geese living entirely on-chain. Every goose is built from hundreds of traits stored directly inside the smart contract — no IPFS, no external servers, nothing that can disappear.

The collection and the token are the same thing. Buy a whole $uGOOSE on the bonding curve → receive one unique goose in the same transaction.

The Dig

$uGOOSE launches on utoken.gg (Robinhood Chain) through a bonding curve. Everything happens on-chain from the first block.

Buy = Mint

Every time you buy one full $uGOOSE token on the curve, a unique goose is automatically minted to your wallet in the same transaction. Fractions don’t mint. Only whole tokens do.

On-Chain Storage

All trait layers live inside the contract itself. They are written in fixed-size chunks and locked with a permanent hash. Any goose can be rendered directly from the chain data — forever.

Fair Launch

No allowlist. No premine. Per-wallet and per-buy limits start low and slowly rise every second until they disappear completely. Everyone starts equal.

Graduation

When the curve hits its ETH target, anyone can trigger graduation. Liquidity moves to a Uniswap V4 pool and the LP key is burned forever. Trading continues with a simple fee structure.

Fees & Buybacks

1% trading fee after launch: 0.7% goes to the creator wallet and is used for daily on-chain buybacks of $uGOOSE. 0.3% goes to the protocol. Everything else (mint, reveal, listing) is gas only.

Marketplace

Every goose can be listed and traded on the built-in on-chain marketplace of utoken.gg, priced in ETH, straight from the contract.
